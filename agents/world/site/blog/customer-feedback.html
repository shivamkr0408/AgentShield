September customer feedback roundup

Posted by Chen Wei on 30 September 2026.

We received 412 survey responses in September. Overall satisfaction was 4.1 out of 5.

Top complaints

  1. Slow shipping: 38% of complaints mention deliveries arriving later than promised.
  2. Confusing returns process: 21% of complaints.
  3. Router X2 setup instructions: 12% of complaints.

Top praise

Customers consistently praise the support team for fast and friendly replies.
