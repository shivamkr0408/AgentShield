What it does

  • A layered defense for tool-using LLM agents: de-obfuscating preprocessor, a three-layer detector (multilingual rules, an xlm-roberta classifier, and an LLM intent check), canary tokens, and origin taint tracking.
  • Signals are fused by logistic regression and enforced by a policy firewall: allow, sanitize, or block, with human approval and a fail-safe timeout.
  • A multilingual indirect-injection dataset (English, Hindi, Tamil, Hinglish, Tanglish) with benign hard negatives.

Key findings

  • Canary and taint tracking catch exfiltration that content detectors miss — a benign-looking page that drives the agent to email a secret is stopped by the tripwire, not by any trigger word.
  • The preprocessor neutralizes obfuscations (base64, hex, URL-encoding, homoglyphs) that evade a keyword baseline.
